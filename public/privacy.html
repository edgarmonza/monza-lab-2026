Privacy Policy

Monza Lab Data Connector — Last updated: June 4, 2026

1. Overview

Monza Lab Data Connector ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Lupa Enterprises LLC ("Monza Lab") to connect our own Meta advertising and Instagram accounts with our analytics systems. The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from third parties.

2. Data We Access

The App accesses the following data exclusively from Monza Lab's own accounts:

  • Ad campaign performance metrics (impressions, reach, clicks, spend)
  • Instagram business account insights (followers, post metrics)
  • Audience demographic breakdowns (age, gender, country — aggregated, never individual)

3. Data Storage

Data retrieved through the App is processed in-memory during active sessions and may be saved as local analytics snapshots on Monza Lab's own systems. No data is transmitted to third-party services, sold, or shared with external parties.

4. Third-Party Data

The App does not access, collect, or process data from any user other than Monza Lab's own business accounts. No end-user data from Meta or Instagram is collected.

5. Data Retention

Analytics snapshots are retained for internal business analysis purposes. Data can be deleted upon request by contacting us at the email below.

6. Data Deletion

To request deletion of any data associated with this App, contact: edgar@monzalab.com. Requests will be processed within 30 days.
